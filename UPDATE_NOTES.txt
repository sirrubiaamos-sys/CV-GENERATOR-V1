═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                   HOW TO USE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. OPEN THE APP
   └─ Double-click index.html

2. ADD PROFILE PHOTO
   └─ Click "Upload Photo" button
   └─ Select an image from your computer
   └─ Photo appears circular in form and CV

3. FILL SIDEBAR INFORMATION
   ├─ Enter personal info (name, email, phone, location)
   ├─ Click "+ Add Language" and select languages
   ├─ Enter nationality
   └─ Click "+ Add" for referees and enter details

4. FILL MAIN FORM
   ├─ Professional summary
   ├─ Education
   ├─ Work experience
   ├─ Skills (comma-separated)
   └─ Certifications

5. REVIEW PREVIEW
   └─ Professional CV with sidebar layout
   └─ Profile photo at top
   └─ All info organized beautifully

6. DOWNLOAD
   └─ PDF (recommended)
   └─ DOCX (editable)
   └─ Print
