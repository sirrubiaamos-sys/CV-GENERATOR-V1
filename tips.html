📚 CV Tips & Best Practices

✍️ Writing Effective Experience Descriptions

Use Action Verbs

Start every description with a powerful action verb. This makes your CV more dynamic and engaging.

🎯 Achieved
Attained, Completed, Exceeded
🛠️ Created
Built, Designed, Developed
📊 Led
Directed, Managed, Oversaw
🚀 Improved
Enhanced, Optimized, Increased
🤝 Collaborated
Coordinated, Partnered, Worked with
📈 Increased
Boosted, Drove, Expanded
Designed and implemented new customer service protocol, reducing response time by 30% and increasing satisfaction scores from 78% to 92%.
Worked on customer service stuff that made things better.

📊 Quantify Your Achievements

Always include numbers, percentages, and metrics to demonstrate the impact of your work.

Managed a team of 8 salespeople, increasing quarterly revenue by $500K and achieving 110% of annual targets.
Managed a sales team that did well.

🎓 Education Section Tips

What to Include:

  • Institution name (University/College)
  • Degree type (B.S., M.A., B.A., etc.)
  • Major/Field of Study
  • Graduation year (or expected graduation)
  • GPA (if 3.5 or higher, and relevant to the job)
  • Relevant coursework (if recent graduate)
  • Honors or achievements (Dean's List, Cum Laude, etc.)
Bachelor of Science in Computer Science
University of California, Berkeley | May 2022
GPA: 3.7/4.0 | Dean's List (All Semesters)
Went to UC Berkeley
Got a degree in Computer Stuff

🎯 Skills Section Guidelines

Best Practices:

  • List 5-15 skills (relevance over quantity)
  • Prioritize skills matching the job description
  • Include both technical and soft skills
  • Be honest - only list skills you actually possess
  • Group related skills together
  • Update regularly based on the job posting
Technical Skills Leadership Project Management Communication Problem Solving

🏆 Professional Summary Tips

Your Summary Should:

  • Be 2-3 sentences maximum
  • Highlight your key strengths
  • Show your career goals and ambitions
  • Use keywords from the job posting
  • Be tailored to each job application
Results-driven Full Stack Developer with 5+ years of experience building scalable web applications. Proven track record of leading cross-functional teams and delivering projects 15% ahead of schedule. Passionate about clean code and continuous learning.
I'm a software developer. I like programming. I'm looking for a job in tech.

✅ CV Formatting Checklist

  • ✓ Use consistent formatting throughout
  • ✓ Keep consistent date formats (e.g., Jan 2022 - Present)
  • ✓ Use a professional font (Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman)
  • ✓ Font size 10-12pt for body text
  • ✓ Maintain 0.5-1 inch margins
  • ✓ Use bold for job titles and company names
  • ✓ Use italics sparingly for emphasis
  • ✓ Avoid graphics and images (unless design-related role)
  • ✓ Save as PDF to preserve formatting
  • ✓ Keep to 1 page (2 pages max for senior professionals)

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

Using personal pronouns (I, me, we). Your CV should speak for itself.
Including salary expectations or reasons for leaving jobs.
Using vague descriptions like "responsible for" or "duties included".
Including irrelevant information (hobbies, age, photo unless required).
Exaggerating or lying about skills or experience. They will fact-check.
Using unprofessional email addresses. Use firstname.lastname@email.com format.
Multiple typos or grammatical errors. Proofread multiple times!

🔍 Before Downloading Your CV

  1. Proofread - Check for typos and grammar errors 3+ times
  2. Spellcheck - Use browser or editor spellcheck
  3. Format Check - Ensure consistent formatting throughout
  4. Verify Accuracy - Double-check all dates, company names, job titles
  5. Tailor Content - Make sure it matches the job posting
  6. Test Download - Download and open the file to verify it looks correct
  7. Review in PDF - PDF version should look professional

🎯 Tailor Your CV for Different Roles

For Each Job Application:

  • Update your professional summary to match the job
  • Reorder experience to highlight most relevant positions
  • Emphasize skills matching the job description
  • Update certifications if relevant
  • Adjust the level of detail based on the role

📱 CV Format Recommendations

PDF Download (Recommended)

✅ Preserves formatting across all devices
✅ Professional appearance
✅ Most commonly requested by employers
❌ Cannot edit after download

DOCX Download (Word Document)

✅ Can edit after download
✅ Works with all office software
⚠️ May have formatting issues on different computers
⚠️ Larger file size

🚀 Advanced Tips

Create multiple versions of your CV tailored to different types of positions.
Use keywords from the job description in your CV. Many companies use ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems).
Keep a master CV with all your experience, then create tailored versions for specific jobs.
Update your CV every time you complete a major project or achievement.
Have someone else review your CV before submitting to employers.

📧 Cover Letter Ideas

Your CV pairs well with a customized cover letter that:

  • Addresses the hiring manager by name
  • Shows enthusiasm for the specific company and role
  • Highlights 2-3 key achievements from your CV
  • Explains why you're a good fit
  • Is kept to 3-4 short paragraphs
