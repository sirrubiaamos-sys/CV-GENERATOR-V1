💡 Pro Tips Before You Start

  • Use Action Verbs: Start descriptions with powerful verbs like "Designed," "Led," "Achieved"
  • Quantify Achievements: Include numbers and percentages to show impact
  • Tailor Your CV: Customize it for each job application using keywords from the posting
  • Proofread Carefully: Check multiple times for typos and grammar errors
  • Keep It Concise: Aim for 1-2 pages maximum
  • Save Regularly: Download and backup your CV in multiple formats
